# ThermalPrint Privacy Policy

Effective: November 23, 2026

ThermalPrint is a local desktop application for designing, preparing, and printing labels. The application and this policy apply to both the Windows and Linux editions.

## Data processing

ThermalPrint processes PDF, image, CSV, design, font, printer, and label data on the user's computer. Documents and designs are not uploaded to a ThermalPrint server.

ThermalPrint does not require an account and does not collect analytics, telemetry, advertising identifiers, location data, or usage data.

## Storage

The app may save:

- User-created `.thermalprint` designs and exported files at locations chosen by the user.
- Label stock profiles, print history, templates, and app preferences on the local computer.
- Temporary files in the operating system's temporary directory while processing documents.

The app may read printer names and settings and the contents of a watch folder selected by the user. These settings and files remain under the control of the operating system and the user.

## Network access

ThermalPrint does not send local document or label content to the project developer. A user-initiated request may open a browser for the project website, source repository, user manual, issue tracker, or optional PayPal donation page. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Third-party services

The app does not currently integrate with carrier, marketplace, payment, or advertising accounts. Optional operating-system printer drivers and other third-party software are governed by their providers' policies.

## Updates and distribution

Distribution platforms may collect their own installation, purchase, update, crash, or device information according to their privacy policies. ThermalPrint itself has no telemetry.

## Contact

Questions or privacy concerns can be submitted at https://github.com/arios4k/thermalprint/issues.
