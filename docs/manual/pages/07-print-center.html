Print Center is where you tell ThermalPrint about your printer. You usually set it up once. The same printer, media, and options are then used by both Import & Convert and the Label Designer.

Printer and media

ThermalPrint prints through CUPS on Linux and the Windows print spooler on Windows 11. Add the printer in your system's printer settings first. On Windows, configure the label stock in the printer driver so ThermalPrint can list its dimensions. See Windows setup.

SettingWhat it means
Printer queueThe printers your system knows. The line below shows a best-effort hint such as Likely thermal label printer. It is based on the name and media, and is a hint only.
Printer media sizeThe paper size the printer driver offers. Choose the one that matches your loaded labels, such as 4 × 6 in. A Custom dimensions choice appears if the driver supports it.
Advanced driver resolutionAn optional resolution choice if the printer offers one. Leave it on Driver default unless you have a reason.
Refresh printersLooks for printers again after you add or turn on a printer.

Why media matters. The media tells the printer driver how big the label is, so it does not shrink, rotate, or add margins. If the output size and the printer media disagree, ThermalPrint warns you and offers Match output to printer media.

Label stock and queue

The Label stock card draws your label's shape and size, with DPI, copies, and the printer media. The Queue & diagnostics card shows:

  • A status notice that says what to do next: Add a label, Review the crop, Choose a printer, Choose printer media, Label size differs from printer media, Verify the loaded stock, or Ready to submit.
  • How many imported labels are queued, with Open import queue to jump there.
  • Job status checks the system print queue. When a job is accepted, check the physical output to confirm it printed correctly.

Alignment and printer profiles

Each printer remembers its own settings

If you use more than one printer, such as a 4 × 6 shipping printer and a small price-tag printer, ThermalPrint remembers the media, resolution, DPI, label size, copies, and alignment separately for each. Switch printers in Printer queue and the right settings come back automatically. A printer you have never used starts clean. No "save" button is needed.

Safe margin for a printer that clips its edges

Under Alignment, Safe margin sets how far designs should stay from the label's edge, from 0 to 10 mm. It is remembered for each printer. In the Label Designer, the canvas shades that border and names any object inside it, even when the printer's driver reports no unprintable border. See Safe margin.

Fixing a printer that prints off-center

Some printers feed labels a little to one side, so the content is shifted or the edge is cut off. The alignment offset moves everything by a small amount to correct this.

  1. Print the test label. Press Print alignment test. It prints one ruler label, even if Copies is set higher, with a thin border 1 mm in from every edge, a cross at the center, and tick marks every 5 mm.
  2. Measure. On a correctly aligned label the border is the same distance from each edge. Measure how far it is shifted.
  3. Enter the correction. Set Horizontal and Vertical in millimeters (positive moves content right and down, negative moves it left and up). The range is −10 to +10 mm.
  4. Print the test again and adjust until the border is even.
{{image:alignment-test|The alignment test label: a thin border with tick marks every five millimeters and a cross at the center|The alignment test label, as printed. The border sits 1 mm inside every edge.}}

Reset offset sets both values back to zero. The offset applies to printed labels only; exported PDF, PNG, SVG, and ZPL files are never shifted. It also does not apply to raw ZPL printing.

If the whole image is too small or the edges are cut off by the same amount all around, the cause is usually the media size, not alignment. Check that the printer media matches your labels first.

Label footer

The Label footer card adds a small line of text along the bottom of every label you print, such as Printed with ThermalPrint or your shop's name. You can also add a small QR code just before the message, for example a link to your website. The footer is off until you turn it on, and when it is off your labels print exactly as before.

  1. In Print Center, open the Label footer card and enable Print a footer message.
  2. Type your Message, up to 80 characters.
  3. To add a QR code, enable Add a QR code beside the message and enter a link or text, up to 200 characters. It starts with a link to this user manual. The QR code prints only with a message.
  4. Choose the Footer position: Left, Center, or Right. The QR code and message move together, with the message centered beside the QR code.
  5. Print one label and scan the QR code with your phone before printing a batch.

The footer applies to imported labels, Label Designer prints, batches, number ranges, printed sheets, reprints, and automatic printing. It never covers the label itself:

  • Imported labels are fitted above a thin strip reserved for the footer, so they shrink slightly. With Actual size or a custom scale, a label that no longer fits is blocked with a warning instead of being covered. Use Fit, reduce the scale, or turn the footer off.
  • Designs keep their layout. The footer goes in the empty strip at the bottom of the label, inside the safe margin. If part of the design is in that strip, the footer is left out and the Label Designer says why. Move the design up to make room.

The footer stays small: the message is about 2 mm tall, and long messages are shortened to fit. The QR code uses modules at least 0.25 mm wide and the full white border scanners need. That border also serves as the space above the footer and overlaps the white margin below it, so a 4 × 6 in label gives up about 8 mm for a footer with a QR code and about 3 mm for a message alone. On short labels, where the QR code would take more than about an eighth of the height, it is left out and only the message prints.

Your footer settings are saved and stay on between sessions. Reset footer turns the footer off and restores the default message and link. The footer appears in the preview and on printed labels only. Exported PDF, PNG, SVG, and ZPL files never include it.

Recent prints

Batch runs and number ranges are recorded once, with the count in the title, for example Customer label · batch of 50 or Shelf tag · numbers 20; reopening one opens the original design, not a single numbered label.

Each label you print is added to Recent prints at the bottom of Print Center, with the date and time, printer, and copies. The app keeps up to 50 entries. Search by label name or printer; Clear beside the search field clears the search, while Clear history removes the entries.

{{figure:print-history|Recent prints in version 0.13.0, with a search field, Open and Reprint buttons, and recorded media, dimensions, DPI and copies|Search recent jobs and choose Open for review or Reprint for a confirmed repeat job.}}
  • Press Open to bring the label back. A design reopens in the Label Designer as an unsaved copy exactly as it was printed; an imported label returns to the queue in Import & Convert.
  • Nothing prints when you press Open. You review the label, then press Print yourself.
  • If the original file has been moved or deleted, you are told to add it again.
  • Clear history removes the list.

A batch print is listed once, as Design name · batch of N. See Folder watching and history for more.

Printer setup assistant and label stock

The Printer setup assistant card is at the top of Print Center. Choose Set up printer and label stock:

  1. Choose the installed printer below the assistant. Use Refresh printers if it is missing.
  2. Measure the loaded label and set its width, height, orientation, driver media, and DPI. Match media to label size selects a matching media size when the driver offers one.
  3. Print one alignment test, compare the 1 mm border to the stock edges, and adjust the offsets below.
  4. Enter a Stock profile name and choose Save stock profile.
{{figure:printer-setup|Printer setup assistant in version 0.13.0, above the printer, media, size and alignment controls|The assistant guides the existing controls below it. This example shows a fictional 4 × 6 printer.}}

Named profiles let the same printer use several kinds of stock. Find the profile under Saved label stock and choose its Load profile button to restore its size, media, driver resolution, DPI, offsets, copies, and safe margin. A profile belongs to its printer; switch to that printer before loading it. Up to 50 named profiles are retained. Close assistant closes the guide; your current printer settings remain in use.

Reprinting from history

Search Recent prints by label name or printer. New entries record their crop, page, print settings, and editable design when small enough to retain. Reprint… shows the recorded settings and asks before sending another job. A changed or missing source file requires you to open it for review. Labels moved to Processed keep their history link. Older entries can still be opened for review.

{{figure:history-reprint|Reprint label confirmation showing the recorded printer, copies, dimensions, DPI and media, with Cancel and Reprint actions|Reprint sends a new job only after confirmation. Cancel sends nothing.}}

The recorded printer must still be available. A saved design snapshot can be reprinted even if its original file is gone; imported labels need the unchanged source file. Reprint… is disabled for entries without a complete recipe, including older entries and batch or numbered runs. Use Open and review those jobs instead. Confirmed reprints are added to history. Clearing history does not reset automatic-print duplicate protection.
