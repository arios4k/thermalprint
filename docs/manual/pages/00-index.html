ThermalPrint is a desktop app for people who print shipping labels, price tags, shelf labels, and inventory stickers. Label files are processed on your computer. ThermalPrint uses CUPS on Linux and the Windows print spooler on Windows 11. You can also use a laser or inkjet printer with supported paper sizes, or place several labels on an A4 or Letter sheet.

See Getting started on Windows 11 for Store availability, printer setup, and your first print. Most screenshots in this manual were captured on Linux.

This manual covers version 0.13.1, including background printing, named stock profiles, confirmed reprints and the new templates. Screenshots follow your browser's light or dark theme. See What's new in 0.13.1 for the release overview.

If a control is missing, check your installed version in About ThermalPrint and update through your installation channel. Microsoft Store updates appear after certification.

Principles

  • Open source. ThermalPrint’s source code, including the Windows code, is available under the MIT license. Linux downloads are free. The Windows Microsoft Store edition is a one-time purchase with a free trial. The app has no account, advertising, or tracking.
  • Private by design. Labels are processed on your computer. The only things that leave it are the print job you send to your printer and the links you click yourself, such as this manual.
  • Uses printers already configured on your system. Choose your printer and a media size it supports in Print Center.
  • Careful before it prints. A label that would be cut off is blocked, and a batch is checked row by row before anything is sent.
  • Keyboard controls. Named controls, shortcuts and numeric position, size and rotation fields help you work without dragging. See Keyboard shortcuts.
  • In your language. The app follows your system language: English, Spanish, German, and French. This manual is in English.

Getting help

  • In the app, press F1 or click the ? button in the title bar to open this manual at the page for the screen you are on.
  • Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue. Please remove personal addresses and tracking numbers from anything you post.
  • ThermalPrint's author also makes two other free Linux apps with manuals in this same style: Voltkit and Battery Health Center.
