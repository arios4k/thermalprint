ThermalPrint is a desktop app for people who print labels on a thermal label printer: shipping labels downloaded from a carrier or marketplace, price tags, shelf labels, inventory stickers, and anything else that fits on a roll. Everything happens on your computer. Your files are never uploaded, and you do not need an account with anyone.

Five-minute quick start

There are two common ways to begin. Pick the one that matches what you have in front of you.

A. You have a shipping label file

  1. Install ThermalPrint and add your printer. See Installing. Add the printer in your desktop's own printer settings first; ThermalPrint prints through the same system.
  2. Open the label. Drag the PDF or image into the window, press Ctrl+O, or choose Add labels… in Import & Convert.
  3. Check the crop. ThermalPrint proposes the label area. Look at the Thermal label preview, and tick I checked the crop and barcode edges when it looks right.
  4. Pick the paper. Choose 4 × 6 in (or your stock size), your printer, and the matching media.
  5. Print. Press Print label or Ctrl+P.

B. You want to make a label

  1. Open the Label Designer and choose a template on the Home screen, or start with a blank label.
  2. Edit the objects. Click text to change it, drag things where you want them, and use the panel on the right for exact sizes.
  3. Choose your printer in Print Center so the label size matches your stock.
  4. Press Print. Save the design (Ctrl+S) if you will use it again.

Print a test label first. A successful submission means the printer system accepted the job. It does not prove that a barcode scans. Always check the first physical label before printing a roll.

Getting help

  • Press F1 or the ? button in the title bar at any time. ThermalPrint opens the manual page for the screen you are on.
  • Use the search box at the top of every page, or press /.
  • Open the main menu (☰) and choose Printing terms for a searchable glossary inside the app.
  • Stuck? Try Troubleshooting, then report a bug on GitHub. Please remove personal addresses and tracking numbers from anything you post.
