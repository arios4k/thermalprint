The sidebar

Click the small panel icon at the top of the sidebar to collapse it to icons only, or expand it again. ThermalPrint remembers your choice the next time you open it. In the Label Designer, Focus hides the sidebar and the properties panel to give the canvas the whole window.

The Home screen

  • Create new label opens the Label Designer with a new blank label.
  • Open existing labels opens the file chooser for PDFs and images.
  • Templates lists ready-made layouts: Blank custom, Shipping 4 × 6, Return address, Product barcode, Inventory, QR code, Asset tracking, Shelf, Price tag, Name label, and Fragile. Hover one to read what it is for. Example text and codes in a template are placeholders; replace them before printing.
  • Printer status shows the selected printer and a hint about it.
  • Recent documents lists files you opened before. Pick one to open it again.

Choose System, Light, or Dark in the main menu. System follows your desktop's setting and changes with it.

Language

ThermalPrint is available in English, Spanish, German, and French. It uses your computer's language automatically: if your system language is one of these, the interface appears in it; otherwise it appears in English. There is no language setting inside the app. To change the language, change your system language and restart ThermalPrint.

{{figure:home-es|The home screen in Spanish|Spanish.}} {{figure:home-de|The home screen in German|German.}} {{figure:home-fr|The home screen in French|French.}}

Most labels, buttons, and explanations are translated. Some status messages, error details, template descriptions, and the printing-terms glossary are still in English. Printer names and the text on your own labels are never changed.

Opening files by drag and drop

Drop files onto the window from your file manager at any time:

  • PDFs and images: they join the print queue in Import & Convert. You can drop several at once.
  • One .thermalprint file: it opens in the Label Designer. If your current design has unsaved changes, you are asked to Save, Discard, or Cancel first.

For a folder that fills up with new labels, see Folder watching and history.
